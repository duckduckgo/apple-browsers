RMF transaction

Require current app/UI, host, window, and gate readiness.
Reconcile any stale exact modal root.
Acquire the singular RMF owner.
Evaluate modal→RMF and RMF→RMF boundaries.
On denial, release raw ownership; retain and account nothing.
On success, publish the admitted session.
First matching card appearance confirms RMF history, then shown accounting.
Final physical removal plus one main turn releases and may hand off.

Modal transaction

Pass existing launch-source and unrelated-modal gates.
Acquire the singular modal owner before provider evaluation.
Evaluate RMF→modal cooldown.
On denial, release raw ownership without querying providers.
On success, enter the existing manager flow.
The manager applies modal→modal policy and owns exact-root lifetime.
