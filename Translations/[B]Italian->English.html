Paris: Chiara Pellacani historic in plein, 5 golds out of 5 at the European diving championships

The blue also wins with Elisa Pizzini the synchrous 3 meters. 'Now I approach the Chinese'

ROME, 06 August 2026, 20:52

by Alessio Corsaro

Share

0:002:08

Ansa EXPLAINER - How the America’s Cup works

X

Never anyone like her.

Chiara Pellacani completes a legendary en plein and conquers the fifth gold on as many finals held at the European swimming championships in Paris, thanks to a perfect race in the synchro springboard from three meters in pairs with Elisa Pizzini, crowned with the score of 308.07. "I was aware of my worth, but in these Europeans I realized the leap in quality," commented the golden girl, who had just come out of the tank, revealing that she imagined herself on the eve of the continental championships "in a photo with all the gold medals around her neck", as confirmed two days ago by her coach, Tommaso Marconi. Presage fulfilled and, despite being fresh from a record with which he reiterated the overtaken to an Italian icon of the discipline such as Tania Cagnotto, the Roman athlete - already now the blue face of these Europeans - now looks at the Los Angeles Olympics in 2028.

"I died at the highest level and now I want to get closer to the Chinese", relaunched Pellacani next to his partner Pizzini, who after the bronze faded in the individual trampoline from three meters redeemed "a year not started in the best way" with a victory "that is worth everything", the result "of the harmony that Chiara and I are building as people and athletes". After dominating the team test, the three-meter mixed synchro, the one-meter trampoline and the three-meter trampoline, Pellacani was the protagonist, along with Pizzini, of a dramatic race, marked by an unexpected self-sabotage of the English Yasmin Harper and Scarlett Mew Jensen, who withdrew from the competition after two consecutive dives from zero points. At that point, not even the Ukrainians and the Germans, respectively silver and bronze medals with 278.40 and 274.59 points, could worry the Italian torque, a pneumatic hammer capable of drawing perfect trajectories from the trampoline. "Before I dived, I told Elisa to go serene and convinced," Pellacani declared. "She understood that and the results were seen. He just has to realize his potential, because together we live up to an Olympic medal."

A fixed thought, moved by the desire to redeem those fourth places in the three-meter female and individual three-meter synchro springboard obtained at the 2024 Paris Olympics, which from tears turned into awareness. Awarded as the best European diver of 2025, "another goal achieved", Chiara has already mortgaged the continental crown also for 2026, tracing a clear path that, with the due proportions, recalls the feelings of total triumphs such as the Football Triplet, the Grand Slam in tennis or the Triple Crown on the two wheels of cycling. A circle that closes for that girl who won her first gold in Glasgow right in the three-metre synchro springboard at the 2018 Europeans, when she was not even 16 years old yet. She has now entered the club of the biggest divers around and the final seal could come in two years' time in Los Angeles, for a new appointment with history.
