Vini Jr. accepts bid and renews contract with Real Madrid until 2032

Brazilian reached an agreement to extend the contract after progress in negotiations between the parties

Gustavo Riguetti, CNN Brazil06/08/26 at 15:03 | Updated 06/08/26 at 15:46
1 of 12
Skip Carousel
End of the carousel

Brazilian striker Vini Jr. had the contract renewal with Real Madrid announced on Thursday afternoon (6). The will of the player, allied to that of coach Jose Mourinho and that of President Florentino Pérez, was fundamental to the conclusion of the agreement until 2032.

The meeting held last Wednesday (5) reinforced the Spanish club's interest in keeping the 7 shirt and marked a change of stance in the negotiations.

Leia mais

Unlike in recent years, both with Vini Jr. and with other athletes, Real Madrid have increased the proposal and presented new conditions to extend the Brazilian bond.

Entering the Group

In addition to entering the final year of contract, Vini Jr. sparked interest from Arsenal, who were willing to invest in his signing. The coach of the reigning Premier League champion, Mikel Arteta, has placed the 26-year-old Brazilian striker as one of the priorities to bolster the squad.

However, the desire to remain at Real Madrid, added to the salary appreciation, the increase in gloves and the protagonism guaranteed by the Spanish club, was decisive for the 7 shirt to hit the renewal for another six seasons.
