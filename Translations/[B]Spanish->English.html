El Real Madrid anuncia uno de los fichajes más caros de su historia y paga en torno a 120 millones de euros por Diomande

  • The 19-year-old Ivorian winger arrives with a contract until 2033 after signing a great season at RB Leipzig
  • From the streets of Abidjan to the Bernabéu passing through Leganés: the story of overcoming the footballer who dreamed of Cristiano
ILIAS BENTADDA SEBTI*

After several weeks of talking about him, Diomande is already a Real Madrid player. The white club continues to bet on young talent and already has its new reinforcement for the attack. The club has made official the signing of the 2006 winger, from RB Leipzig, in an operation valued at around 120 million euros and with a contract until 2033. Just a year after leaving Leganés for 20 million euros of transfer, the footballer returns to the Spanish capital become one of the great sensations of European football after an outstanding season in the Bundesliga, where he was chosen best debutant of the championship thanks to his 12 goals and nine assists.

Their hiring puts an end to one of the soap operas on the market. Paris Saint-Germain reached a verbal agreement with the player during the World Cup, although it never managed to meet the economic demands of Leipzig. Liverpool also matched Real Madrid's offer, but Diomande maintained the commitment he had made to the merengues and prioritized wearing the Madrid shirt above the rest of the proposals. A bet that confirms that Real Madrid’s strategy is to get ahead of the competition to get some of the biggest talents on the international scene.

De Abiyán a Europa

Raised on the streets of Abidjan, he left his home while still a child to enter the Inter Foot Sud Comoé academy, near the border with Ghana, and thus pursue the dream of becoming a professional footballer. At the age of 15, he left his family behind to travel to the United States, where he landed at the Florida DME Academy without speaking English and facing a completely different culture for the first time.

Those two years marked his character. The footballer himself has acknowledged that it was difficult to adapt to the American language, food and way of life. While many of the great talents of his generation grew up in quarries of the main European clubs, he continued to train in Daytona Beach and led the modest AS Frenzi until conquering the United Premier Soccer League (professional league that brings together regional categories). His performance caught the attention of the Leganés, who bet on him in January 2025. Just a few months later, Leipzig paid about 20 million euros to incorporate them, and only one season later, Real Madrid have decided to turn it into one of the faces of their future.

La carta a su hermana y CR7

Beyond football, Diomande has thrilled by his personal history. In a letter dedicated to his late sister Roxane, he recalled a childhood marked by family hardship and sacrifice. He told how he wrote "Ronaldo 7" with a marker on a fake Manchester United shirt because the Portuguese star was his great reference, how he waited for everyone to fall asleep to watch football in silence or how he came to go hungry in an academy away from home. "I slept with my first football boots because I had never had one," he recalled in one of the most emotional passages in a letter collected by The Players Tribune.

Qué puede aportar al Real Madrid

On the grass, Diomande stands out for being an electric, vertical and aggressive end in the one against one. You can play in both bands, although you usually leave from the left to take advantage of your speed and attack spaces. His physical power, overflow capacity make him a profile that can complement the same skills offered by players such as Vinicius or Kylian Mbappé within the team of José Mourinho, in addition to being a footballer very involved in the pressure and without fear of assuming responsibilities.

The boy who dreamed of Cristiano Ronaldo will have the opportunity to follow in his footsteps and in the same stadium where his idol wrote an unforgettable part of the history of football, Yan Diomande will have the opportunity to write the most important chapter of his career at Real Madrid.
