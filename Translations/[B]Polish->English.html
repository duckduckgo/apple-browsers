Electric bicycle - is it worth it? The key to success is batteries - how to deal with them to be reliable

Bartosz Biskupski
You can read this in 4 minutes
April 22, 2026, 14:17 PM
  • Subskrybuj nas na YouTube
  • Zapisz się na newsletter
Elektromobilność to nie tylko samochody czy hulajnogi i podobne im urządzenia. Rośnie także segment e-bike, czyli swojskie rowery elektryczne. Z jednej strony są wymarzonym sprzętem sportowym, z drugiej co raz częściej narzędziem prowadzenia biznesu. Zamówienie z restauracji, szybka dostawa dokumentów w centrum, co raz częściej dostarczyciel przybywa rowerem elektrycznym.
Electromobility. Are electric bikes good business or just a niche recreation? / Forsal.pl
Electromobility is not only about cars or scooters and devices similar to them. The e-bike segment, i.e. home-made electric bicycles, is also growing. On the one hand, they are a dream sports equipment, on the other hand, more and more often a tool for running a business. Order from the restaurant, fast delivery of documents in the center, more and more often the supplier arrives by electric bike.
  • Electric bicycles in Poland are good business?
  • Long life and real scenarios in which the battery works
  • Battery, how to make sure to maintain coverage after winter?
  • Electric bike: what to do when the battery goes down

The electric bicycle market (e-bike) in Poland is developing dynamically. It is estimated that by 2025 the number of users of electric bicycles in Poland amounted to about 600 thousand people.
Sales are an important indicator of market development. In 2022, about 70 thousand electric bicycles were sold in Poland. E-bike is an increasingly popular solution. After a period of dynamic growth during the pandemic and the natural market correction, the electric bike segment remains the fastest growing part of the industry today.
“For us, as a company that has been focused on battery technologies since the beginning – development towards batteries for e-bikes is a natural step and consistent implementation of our strategy in the field of electromobility,” says Paweł Ochyński, CEO of Greencell.

Electric bicycles in Poland are good business?

Forecasts assume an increase to more than 150,000 units a year in 2025-2026. With the increase in sales, the importance of e-bikes in the entire bicycle market is also growing – already in 2023 they accounted for about 25% of the market value, and according to forecasts they can reach about 28% by 2026. The current value of the e-bike market in Poland is estimated at about 1.25 billion PLN in the years 2023-2024, with an expected increase to about 2 billion PLN after 2025. Based on this data, experts estimate that the total number of electric bicycles used in Poland is currently about 0.5-1 million units.

Long life and real scenarios in which the battery works

The battery life depends on the way of use, but in a typical scenario – e.g. daily commutes to work (approx. 10 km per day) and weekend trips – the annual mileage can be about 10 000 km. Assuming an average of 80 km on a single charging cycle, this means about 125 cycles per year.
In comparison, in practice, this translates into a value of about 8 years until the capacity drops to 80% in laboratory conditions or about 4 years under real conditions of use – the moment when the capacity decrease becomes noticeable.
“We are currently working on new solutions, including a smart battery management system (SMARS) that will enable users to access detailed parameters through a mobile application. The plans also include expanding the compatibility of the battery by increasing the maximum power of the battery, capacity and the possibility of use in systems with regenerative braking – says Michał Marec, engineer of battery packet designer, Greencell.

Battery, how to make sure to maintain coverage after winter?

The range of the electric bike is influenced by many factors to avoid significant loss of parameters, experts from Greencell recommend that after the winter and after the first launch of the e-bike, perform several activities. First, move the battery to room temperature 2-3 hours before charging, check the battery status. Whether there are damages or Second, the first charge is done to full, 100%.
Third time, the new season starts with a shorter tour. The test whether the battery works for sure properly and which will save the forced way back with unforeseen physical effort, often in beautiful but very demanding off-road circumstances.

After the season for long-term storage, it is recommended to charge about 50%, temperature approx. 20°C and disconnecting the battery from the bike.
The waste batteries should be disposed of to the points of selective collection of waste (PSZOK) or to another point of collection of lithium-ion cells, where they are properly processed

Electric bike: what to do when the battery goes down

After leaving the old, used battery in PSZOK, you can order another one. In the optimal set, the cyclist should find a set containing: a battery, a charger, a key for an anti-theft lock, an instruction and all the elements necessary for mounting in a bicycle.
It is a plug & play solution that allows you to quickly prepare your e-bike for the season.
